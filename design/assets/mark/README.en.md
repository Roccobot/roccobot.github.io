# Mark

Roccobot's personal mark, drawn as a path, in three colors: `tian-turchese.svg` (`#43B59E`, the main form, on a dark background), `tian-petrolio.svg` (`#00727B`, on a light background) and `tian-menta.svg` (`#EBFFF7`, on petrol). The path is the one from the hub's `feedback/` page, byte for byte: it is never modified, only its color changes. The frame (`viewBox`) holds the whole drawing and has the center of the ideal square at its center, the weighted centering of the manual. (The Italian sheet is `README.md`, and it wins if the two disagree.)
