# Icons

AIV's icon in both themes, as a full 108 square (Android's adaptive icon, with no shape: the phone does the masking). `aiv-giorno.svg`, the light theme: mint `#EBFFF7` on turquoise `#43B59E`; `aiv-notte.svg`, the dark theme: light turquoise `#4FD9BE` on petrol `#00727B`. The glyph is the one in `ic_launcher_foreground.xml` of the AIV repo, with its 0.65 scale and its 18.9 translation, unchanged.

The glyph's centering is the one in the manual (the design system's `README.md`, 'Iconography', and `centratura-icona-aiv` in `tokens.json`): it rises by 8.9494% of its own height. In these files, as in the app, it rises by 8.33% (3.25 of 39 units): the rule calls for a vertical translation of 18.66 instead of 18.9, and the files are aligned only when the owner decides. (The Italian sheet is `README.md`, and it wins if the two disagree.)
