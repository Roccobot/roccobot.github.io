# Tondo

The main command of both apps: a round, translucent button in the thumb's corner that opens the commands of the screen. (The Italian sheet is `README.md`, and it wins if the two disagree.)

- **Sizes**: `tondo` (44) in diameter, `tondo-fianco` (24) from the side and `tondo-basso` (16)
  from the bottom, on the side chosen in `Preferred button position`. The touch area is 48.
- **Look**: translucent, with the glyph of the app's mark; the minimum contrast rules are those of
  AIV's button. It is the same button in both apps: only the glyph changes.
- **Two gestures**: a tap opens the screen menu; a long press changes the size of the content (in
  AIV the grid size, in Aomidori the text size or the fit of the page). A help overlay explains the
  two gestures.
- **In the Aomidori reader** it disappears after 2 seconds, a tap brings it back and scrolling
  hides it. After a cross-reference it stretches into a pill with `‹` on the inner side.
- **With TalkBack** it is announced as `Comandi` (Commands).
- The consumer provides the app's glyph and the action of the two gestures.
